Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At Terral Inversiones Globales we respect your privacy and treat your information confidentially. This policy explains what we collect through this website, why, and what rights you have.

1. Who we are

Terral Inversiones Globales, based in Panama City, Panama. For anything regarding your data, write to info@terral.global.

2. What we collect

Only the information you voluntarily provide through the contact form:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Anything you include in your message

This site uses no cookies and no analytics or tracking tools, and collects no browsing data for advertising.

3. How we use it

We use your information solely to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to manage a possible professional relationship. We do not use it for advertising, and we never sell it or share it with third parties for commercial purposes.

4. Legal basis

By submitting the form, you consent to the processing of your data for the purposes described.

5. Who we share it with

To run the site and deliver your messages we rely on providers acting on our behalf: Vercel (site hosting) and Resend (form email delivery). These providers may process the data on servers located outside Panama, for example in the United States.

6. Retention

We keep your information only for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and manage the relationship; after that we delete it.

7. Security

The site is served entirely over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and your data is transmitted securely.

8. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, and withdraw your consent, at any time by writing to info@terral.global.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date indicates the current version.

This policy is governed by Law 81 of 2019 on Personal Data Protection of the Republic of Panama and, where applicable, by the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
